CYBER SAIL

College of Coastal Georgia’s Applied Cybersecurity Club

Hands-on cybersecurity, Capture-the-Flag competitions, and a crew that learns together. Open to all majors and experience levels.

What we do

Learn

Beginner-friendly workshops on web exploitation, forensics, cryptography, password cracking, OSINT, and more. No experience required.

Compete

We play jeopardy-style CTFs as a team — National Cyber League, NSA Codebreaker, and weekend CTFs from around the country.

Get hired

General-interest meetings cover the latest security news, certifications, job hunting, and interview prep so you leave with a résumé that stands out.

Ethical hacker policy

Everything we do is legal and ethical. You must have explicit permission to test any system. All club activities happen on sanctioned, controlled platforms built for this purpose. Unauthorized or illegal activity gets you removed from the club and reported to the college. Every competition has its own rules — read them.
